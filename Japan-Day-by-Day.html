Japan, day by day

17 Feb to 3 Mar 2027 · Tokyo · Kanazawa · Kyoto

Fifteen days of sights and food. Browse a city to see everything there at once, or switch to the suggested plan for each day. Every stop has a Pin and a Navigate button that open the Google Maps app on the exact spot, so they work with the offline maps you downloaded.

  • By city everything in one place, in any order
  • Suggested day a plan for each date
  • Food-led the day built around the best meals
  • Walk-led the day built around the best walking
  • Together seats all three
  • Turns counter only, one eats while one walks
  • Near me reads the phone's GPS and sorts everything by distance
View

Pin opens the stop in the Google Maps app by its exact coordinates and place ID; Navigate asks Maps for walking directions to it; Open as a route strings a whole day plan together in order. All three use the offline maps you downloaded for the pin and the map itself, though Google only gives driving directions without a signal, so for walking use the pin together with the plotted map in each city. Near me uses the phone's GPS, which also works offline. Restaurant hours and scores were read from Tabelog on 11 Oct 2026. Sight opening days are from general knowledge, so recheck them along with closing days in early February.

Woodblock-style artwork was generated with OpenArt. Photographs are from Wikimedia Commons under the licence shown beside each one. Dish photos marked as typical examples show the kind of dish, not that restaurant's own plate. Images load from the web, so they need a connection. The music is generated in your browser and works offline.
